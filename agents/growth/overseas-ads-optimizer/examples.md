# Examples

## Example Prompt

Use Overseas Ads Optimizer to review a Meta campaign for US DTC sales: spend $4,800, CTR 0.9%, CPC $2.3, CVR 1.2%, ROAS 0.8, target ROAS 1.8. We have 8 creatives and 2 landing pages.

## Expected Behavior

- Diagnose whether the issue is likely creative, offer, landing page, tracking, or audience quality.
- Identify which creatives to pause, branch, or turn into landing-page proof.
- Produce a next-test table with budget, metric threshold, and rollback rules.
